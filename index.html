FAQ

Before you message us.

What does 1-on-1 mentorship actually include?

We start by diagnosing your weak areas, then work directly with you to help you truly grasp those topics — not just memorize them. Sessions stay focused on high-yield content, and we track your NBME scores over time to make sure the plan is actually moving the needle.

Is StepUp only for USMLE Step 1?

Our current focus is USMLE Step 1 mentorship. If you're on a different track, message us anyway — we'll tell you honestly whether we can help.

How much does mentorship cost?

It depends on how much support you need. We'll walk you through the options on your free demo call — no pressure to commit.

Is mentorship online or in person?

Both are possible depending on your location and preference. We'll figure out what works best for you during the demo call.

How long does a mentorship cycle run?

It's built around your timeline to your exam date, with weekly check-ins throughout. We'll set a realistic schedule together once we know where you're starting from.

What if I don't know where to start?

That's exactly what the diagnostic step is for. You don't need a plan walking in — we build one with you.
